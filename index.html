Yurist AI

AI-Powered Legal Assistant & Specialist Directory

1 / 5

📱 About the Project

Yurist AI is a smart legal assistant designed specifically for the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan. Powered by artificial intelligence, the app helps users get immediate, high-accuracy legal advice, draft documents like applications and complaints, search the official Lex.uz legislative database, and connect directly with verified legal specialists across multiple categories (Notaries, Advocates, Family, Labor, Business, Property, Tax, and Administrative law).

✨ Key Features

  • Instant AI-powered legal consultations based on Uzbekistan legislation
  • Automated generation of legal applications and complaints ("Ariza va shikoyatlar")
  • Integrated legislation search querying the official Lex.uz database
  • Legal specialists directory categorized by field of law for direct consultations
  • Beautiful fluid Light/Dark theme support with responsive components
  • Verified security and complete data privacy for sensitive legal data

🛠 Tech Stack

Flutter Dart BLoC State Management Clean Architecture REST API & Lex.uz Parsing Hive (NoSQL Database) Secure Storage
